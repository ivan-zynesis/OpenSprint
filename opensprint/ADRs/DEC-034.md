# DEC-034: `/opsp:backlog` Persists; Explore Writes Nothing

## Question

[[DEC-025]] narrowed explore's guardrail from "no artifacts" to "the initiative and only the initiative", because thinking that survives only in a conversation is lost when the conversation ends. Is a tool quietly saving as it goes the right way to get persistence?

## Decision

No. **`/opsp:explore` reverts to writing nothing** — its original guardrail. A new tool, **`/opsp:backlog`**, flushes the in-memory brainstorm to the persistent layer as an initiative at `state: exploring`, holding the Objective and Goal as they formed.

This supersedes [[DEC-025]].

The step function splits, and both paths stay open:

```
  1.   explore -------------------------------> propose
                   (straight through, as today)

  2.   explore --> backlog ..... time ..... triage --> propose
                   persisted                 picked up
```

`/opsp:backlog` may also fire mid-conversation, as a checkpoint.

The entry point must accept **a few sentences of real-world context** and draft the Objective and Goal on the author's behalf. A stakeholder filing an idea never sees OGSM.

## Consideration Factors

| Factor | Weight | Explicit flush verb | Explore saves as it goes ([[DEC-025]]) | No persistence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thinking is not lost | HIGH | Persisted on demand ✓ | Persisted ✓ | Lost ✗ |
| Operator decides what is worth keeping | HIGH | Explicit ✓ | The tool decides ✗ | — |
| Explore stays pure thinking | HIGH | Original guardrail intact ✓ | Compromised ✗ | Intact ✓ |
| Mid-session checkpointing | MED | A verb can fire any time ✓ | Only meaningful at the end ✗ | — |
| Tool count | LOW | One more ✗ | None ✓ | None ✓ |

## Rationale

[[DEC-025]] was a compromise reached because persistence had nowhere else to live. Given somewhere else, the compromise is unnecessary: explore's original guardrail was right, and what was actually missing was **a deliberate flush at the end of it**.

An explicit verb beats passive saving on the thing that matters most here — the operator decides when a thought is worth keeping. Most exploration is not. A tool that saves as it goes either keeps everything, which fills the backlog with noise, or guesses, which is worse.

It also enables something passive saving cannot: firing mid-session, when one idea crystallises and the conversation continues past it.

The entry-point requirement comes from [[DS-HATS-AS-CONTEXT]]. The single interaction a non-technical stakeholder has is an agent reading the board and filing an idea on their behalf. If that path requires understanding OGSM, it does not exist.

## Invalidation Trigger

If operators consistently finish an exploration and forget to run `/opsp:backlog`, the explicit verb is not working and explore should prompt for it on exit. If the backlog fills with items nobody ever triages, the problem is at [[DEC-035]]'s terminal state, not here.
