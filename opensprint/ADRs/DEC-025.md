# DEC-025: `/opsp:explore` Persists the Initiative and Only the Initiative

> **Superseded by [[DEC-034]].** The compromise below was reached because persistence had
> nowhere else to live. `/opsp:backlog` gives it somewhere, so explore reverts to its
> original guardrail: it writes nothing.

## Question

`/opsp:explore` deliberately writes nothing — *"No artifacts. This is thinking time."* [[DS-PROGRESSIVE-CRYSTALLIZATION]] requires work to persist before it is proposal-ready. Does the guardrail lift, narrow, or stay?

## Decision

It **narrows**. `/opsp:explore` writes the initiative descriptor, at `state: exploring`, holding the Objective and Goal as they form — and nothing else.

Driver-specs and ADRs remain `/opsp:propose`-only. The initiative is created on the first substantive exploration and updated as the session develops; a session reaching no proposal still leaves a readable initiative behind.

## Consideration Factors

| Factor | Weight | Initiative only | Lift entirely | Keep: no artifacts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thinking is not lost | HIGH | Persisted ✓ | Persisted ✓ | Lost with the session ✗ |
| No premature truth claims | HIGH | A container claims nothing ✓ | Half-formed driver-specs enter the graph ✗ | None ✓ |
| [[DEC-006]] and [[DEC-009]] untouched | HIGH | Records still propose-only ✓ | Record creation moves upstream ✗ | Untouched ✓ |
| Exploration stays exploratory | MED | Nothing to defend ✓ | Crystallizes early ✗ | Fully open ✓ |
| Noise in `initiatives/` | LOW | Abandoned stubs accumulate ✗ | Worse ✗ | None ✓ |

## Rationale

The guardrail's reasoning is sound: a premature record is a false claim, and the decision graph should not fill with half-formed constraints that something later depends on.

But that reasoning protects **records**, and an initiative is not one. [[DS-PROGRESSIVE-CRYSTALLIZATION]] draws the line: a record claims truth, a container tracks work. An initiative at `state: exploring` asserts only that someone is thinking about something, which is both true and useful, and nothing can `depends-on` it.

So the guardrail does not need to lift to get persistence. Narrowing it from "no artifacts" to "no *records*" gets all of the benefit and keeps every protection.

The accepted cost is abandoned stubs in `initiatives/`. That is acceptable and arguably useful — an exploration that went nowhere is itself worth having recorded, and the board can filter by state. If it becomes noise, stale `exploring` initiatives are a harvestable open loop rather than a reason to stop writing them.

## Invalidation Trigger

If explore-stage initiatives are routinely cited by other records as though they were decided, the container/record distinction is not holding in practice and needs enforcement rather than convention.
