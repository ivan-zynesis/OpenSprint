# DEC-014: compact Is the Single Compile Engine; architecture.md Becomes a Sibling Output

## Question

`architecture.md` is already a compiled view of the driver-specs and ADRs. Is it an input to `compact`, or something `compact` produces?

## Decision

`architecture.md` is a **sibling output** of `compact`, not an input to it. Both the hat views and `architecture.md` compile from the same source: the driver-specs and ADRs.

`compact` becomes the single compile engine. `/opsp:archive`'s compile step, the final phase of `/opsp:knockdown`, and the post-reconciliation rebuild in `/opsp:rebase` and `/opsp:abandon` all call it rather than each synthesising independently.

## Consideration Factors

| Factor | Weight | Sibling output | Input to compact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fidelity | HIGH | One hop from the record ✓ | Compacting a summary compounds loss — `DEC-062` and `DEC-064` flattened twice ✗ |
| Consistency across skills | HIGH | One engine, one set of rules ✓ | Each skill synthesises its own way ✗ |
| Coherence between outputs | MED | Both derive from the same inputs at the same time ✓ | The two can disagree ✗ |

## Rationale

Compacting an already-compacted document is the failure mode [[DEC-011]] rejects, applied at a larger scale. `architecture.md` is one rendering of the record for a particular audience; the hat views are others. They are peers.

Consolidating on one engine also means `--check` covers every compiled artifact, and improving the compiler improves archive, knockdown and reconciliation at once.

## Invalidation Trigger

If `architecture.md` and the hat views need materially different synthesis strategies, they may warrant separate renderers — but they must still share the record as their single input.
