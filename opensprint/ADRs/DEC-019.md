# DEC-019: Measure Anatomy Renders as Optional Sub-Blocks Under `## Measures`

## Question

[[DS-MEASURE-ANATOMY]] gives every measure four parts. Where do bar, posture, evidence and exposure live — as separate records, as frontmatter, or inside the record that states the measure?

## Decision

They are optional third-level headings inside the existing `## Measures` section of the record that owns the goal:

```markdown
## Measures

- **Primary**: <the measure>

### Bar
<position on the spectrum, what it traded, the neighbour not taken>

### Posture
<met, partially met, or the honest position where no bar is set>

### Evidence
<what artifact asserts it — a rule, a scan, an audit output>

### Exposure
<residual risk, who it reaches>
mitigated-by: <initiative-id>
```

All four are optional. A record with none still parses and still satisfies `constraint-unmeasurable` as it does today.

Three new harvest rules follow: `measure-unasserted` (a measure with no Evidence), `measure-unexposed` (no Exposure), `bar-without-neighbour` (a Bar that does not state the position it did not take).

## Consideration Factors

| Factor | Weight | Sub-blocks under `## Measures` | Separate records with `role: bar` | Frontmatter fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Backward compatible | HIGH | Purely additive; old records parse ✓ | Requires migrating existing bars ✗ | Additive ✓ |
| Attaches to what it describes | HIGH | Lives with its measure ✓ | A measure and its bar in two files ✗ | Lives with its record ✓ |
| Holds prose | HIGH | Markdown body ✓ | Markdown body ✓ | YAML is hostile to paragraphs ✗ |
| Reuses existing harvest | MED | `## Measures` is already read ✓ | New record kind to index ✗ | New parse path ✗ |
| Makes structural findings mechanical | MED | `bar-without-neighbour` harvestable ✓ | Harvestable ✓ | Harvestable ✓ |
| A measure as a first-class node | LOW | No ✗ | Yes ✓ | No ✗ |

## Rationale

`DEFAULT_ROLES` already reasons that *"a measure is a property of a goal, not a node of its own"*, and that holds even now that measures are becoming external standards. A SOC2 clause has its own identity in the world, but within this surrogate it is still something a goal is measured by — it does not constrain independently.

Separate records were specifically rejected because they are what this project is migrating *away* from. `DS-HIGH-IMPACT-OPS` and `DS-BACKWARD-COMPAT` carry `role: bar` today, which made `bars` a top-level section and left `evidence` and `exposure` as sections with nothing in them. That is the visible symptom of the modelling error: those two were never top-level concerns, they are facets that only mean something attached to a specific measure. As whole sections they had nothing to be about.

Sub-blocks also convert a synthetic finding into a deterministic one, which is [[DEC-013]]'s direction. `squad/maintainer.md` currently hand-notes *"Neither bar states its neighbour"* — the rendering skill noticed that. Structured sub-blocks make it `bar-without-neighbour`, harvested by code.

Both existing bars migrate cleanly, which is the test that this is not theory. `DS-HIGH-IMPACT-OPS` becomes an `integrity & correctness` goal whose strategy is "mutating reconciliation is operator-invocable only", with its bar, trade and unrecorded neighbour as sub-blocks. `DS-BACKWARD-COMPAT` becomes `change & quality` the same way.

## Invalidation Trigger

If a bar is routinely shared by several goals, it is a node rather than a property and should become a record with its own id. If `mitigated-by` needs to appear anywhere other than inside `### Exposure`, revisit whether it belongs in frontmatter.
