# DEC-041: Posture Is the Judgement; Evidence and Exposure Are Conditional On It

## Question

[[DEC-019]] gives a measure four parts, and the harvest reports `measure-unasserted` and
`measure-unexposed` independently — so every populated measure wants both evidence *and* exposure,
whatever the verdict. On this repository that produced 35 loops, most of them noise.

## Decision

**Posture is an active judgement.** Evidence and exposure are what that judgement requires:

| Posture | Requires | Loop when missing |
|---|---|---|
| `met` | **evidence** | `measure-unasserted` |
| `partially met` · `not met` · `cannot assess` | **exposure** | `measure-unexposed` |
| *absent* | an attestation at all | `measure-unattested` *(new)* |

A measure with no posture is no longer reported as lacking evidence and exposure. It is reported as
**unattested**, which is the accurate finding and a different one.

`measure-unattested` fires only where a measure states something to attest — a populated
`## Measures` with no `### Posture`.

## Consideration Factors

| Factor | Weight | Conditional on posture | Both always required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each loop means one thing | HIGH | A precise finding per verdict ✓ | "Missing evidence" on a measure nobody judged ✗ |
| Signal-to-noise | HIGH | One loop per unattested measure ✓ | Two, neither actionable ✗ |
| Matches how the judgement is made | HIGH | Evidence supports a pass; exposure records a miss ✓ | Treats them as peers ✗ |
| An unmet bar still reports | HIGH | As `measure-unexposed` ✓ | ✓ |
| Rule simplicity | LOW | Three branches ✗ | Two unconditional checks ✓ |

## Rationale

The parts were never peers. **Evidence is what you cite when you are claiming the bar held;
exposure is what you record when you are not.** Demanding both regardless treats a passing measure
as deficient for not documenting a failure that did not happen, and a failing one as deficient for
not citing evidence of a success it did not claim.

The practical consequence is larger than the tidiness. Thirty-five loops on a repository where
*nothing* had been attested drowned the findings that mattered — and a backlog nobody can read is a
backlog nobody triages, which is the failure [[DEC-023]] already warns about for gating.

`measure-unattested` is the loop that should have existed from the start. "This measure has never
been judged" is a different and more urgent statement than "this measure lacks evidence", and the
old rules could not make it.

## Invalidation Trigger

If a `met` posture routinely also needs an exposure — residual risk accepted alongside a pass — then
exposure is unconditional after all and only evidence is conditional. Watch for attestations writing
an exposure under a `met` verdict; that is the signal.
