# DEC-005: Abandoned Universe Archive Structure

## Question

How should the loser universe be preserved after `/opsp:abandon` completes?

## Decision

The abandoned universe is preserved at `opensprint/abandoned/{name}/` containing:
- `snapshot/` — full copy of the loser's `opensprint/` directory at time of abandonment
- `migration-manifest.md` — complete traversal record: what was migrated, what was dropped, classification of every citizen, and the reasoning behind each decision

The loser's git worktree and branch are removed after the archive is written and the operator confirms.

## Archive Structure

```
opensprint/abandoned/{universe-name}/
  snapshot/
    ADRs/           ← loser's ADRs at abandonment time
    driver-specs/   ← loser's driver-specs at abandonment time
    initiatives/    ← loser's initiative descriptors
    architecture.md
  migration-manifest.md
```

## migration-manifest.md Contents

- Abandonment date and source/target universe names
- For each initiative traversed: final classification and reason
- For each ADR traversed: classification, what it mapped to in the winner (if REDUNDANT/SUPERSEDED), or why it was dropped (if CONFLICT)
- For each active change traversed: same
- Summary counts

## Consideration Factors

- **Audit trail**: teams making hard-fork decisions need a record of what was considered and why
- **Recovery path**: if the wrong universe was abandoned, the snapshot enables reconstruction
- **Blockchain analogy**: the abandoned archive is the orphaned chain — it stops producing blocks but its history is preserved

## Invalidation Trigger

If abandoned archives accumulate and consume significant disk space, a flag to skip the snapshot (manifest-only) can be introduced for teams that trust git history alone.
