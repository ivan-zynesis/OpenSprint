## Why

The last milestone of `compact-surrogate`.

`PHILOSOPHY.md` and `README.md` were written before any of this existed, and they are still the source of truth for what OpenSprint *is*. That is now false in a specific way: the objective, the goals and the strategies are records (`DS-AGENTIC-SDLC`, `DS-FULL-COVERAGE`, `DS-DOCUMENTATION-AS-OUTPUT`), and `opensprint/squad/product.md` states them as a compiled view.

Two documents claiming to describe the same thing is exactly the drift this initiative exists to remove. And `DS-DOCUMENTATION-AS-OUTPUT` makes the position explicit: documentation is compiled, never a second effort.

The resolution is not to delete them. `PHILOSOPHY.md` is **freed** to be what it always was — the origin story — precisely because the surrogate now holds the current truth.

## What Changes

- **`PHILOSOPHY.md`** becomes the origin story, marked as history. What the project set out to do, what it got right, and why the ambition grew past it. It stops claiming to state current principles.
- **`README.md`** states what OpenSprint is now and **points at the compiled views** rather than restating them. Entry point, not a second surrogate.
- The six principles are revisited against `DS-AGENTIC-SDLC`. Three hold, two need reframing, and one was answered by the work rather than by argument.
